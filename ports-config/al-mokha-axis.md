#### 📌 Module A: The Classic Operational Configuration Plan (5 Simultaneous Berths)
*   **🚢 Mega-Vessel Marine Berths (Sovereign Class):** Strategic construction of **5 ultra-large container berths operating in absolute parallel**, engineered to accept and clear the world's largest next-gen vessels (Megamax-24 / ULCV Class) with a total displacement capacity of 24,000 TEU per vessel 24/7/365.
*   **🏗️ Ship-to-Shore (STS) Quay Cranes:** Allocation of **8 automated super quay cranes operating in parallel on a single vessel simultaneously** (totaling **40 Super Post-Panamax STS Units** across the 5 berths). These cranes achieve a synchronized handling velocity of **65+ moves/hour per crane**, scaling collective berth throughput to 520 moves/hour and compressing total vessel turnaround time from legacy 109.4 hours down to a strict **76.9 hours only**.
*   **🤖 Automated Stacking Cranes (ASC):** To digest massive vertical stacking yard flows, each dynamic STS crane is programmatically interlocked with 3.5 automated yard cranes, necessitating a baseline deployment of **140 Automated Stacking Cranes (ASC)** running 100% autonomously for high-density slot shuffling, automated container re-handling, and predictive congestion mitigation.
*   **🚢 Autonomous Marine Tugboats:** Deployment of **12 crewless, automated marine tugboats operating on zero-human crews**, guided by synchronized laser arrays, long-range LiDAR arrays (*Sick AG*), and bathymetric sub-surface telemetry to execute millimeter-precision automated vessel alignment and instant harbor docking.

#### 🚀 Module B: The Expanded Super-Mega Operational Strategy (8 Simultaneous Berths)
*   **🚢 Expanded Mega-Vessel Marine Berths:** Structural expansion to **8 ultra-large container berths operating in absolute parallel** to accept and clear 8 mega-vessels daily (averaging 2,917 vessels annually), optimized for net single-vessel discharge loads of exactly 24,000 TEU net movements without redundant positioning cycles.
*   **🏗️ Expanded Ship-to-Shore (STS) Quay Crane Fleet:** Allocation of **8 automated super quay cranes in parallel per single vessel** (totaling **64 Super Post-Panamax STS Units** across the 8 berths). Operating at a stable peak of **65+ moves/hour per crane**, total vessel turnaround time for a maximum-displacement vessel is compressed to a strict **46.1 hours (approx. 1.9 days) only**, cutting vessel port residency by 19.5 hours to engineer the fastest maritime container rotation loop globally.
*   **🤖 Expanded Automated Stacking Crane Fleet:** The 64 synchronized STS crane blocks are programmatically interlocked with 3.5 yard cranes per unit, necessitating a baseline terminal deployment of **224 Automated Stacking Cranes (ASC)** running 100% autonomously under the integrated operating matrices of *PSA International* and *Eurogate / APM Terminals* for high-density slot shuffling.
*   **🛸 Autonomous Guided Vehicles (AGV / Autonomous Ground Carriers):** Provisioning of **5 laser-guided AGV ground units for each individual STS quay crane** (totaling **320 Autonomous Guided Vehicles (AGVs)** for the entire terminal matrix) to route containers instantly from the berth to the ASC blocks under a zero-latency connected truck platooning framework powered by *Schneider Electric* solid-state battery infrastructures.
