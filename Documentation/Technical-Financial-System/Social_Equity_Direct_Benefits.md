## 📊 1. The 20-40-40 Distribution Formula | معادلة التوزيع العادل
To ensure stability and prevent corruption, **AWSAN AI** automates revenue allocation:
*   **40% (Demographic Weight):** Distributed based on population density to ensure every citizen benefits.
*   **40% (Poverty Alleviation):** Directed specifically to regions with the highest poverty rates and lowest infrastructure.
*   **20% (Service Deficit):** Injected into areas with severe shortages in electricity, water, and healthcare (e.g., Al-Jawf, Saada, Raymah).

## 💳 2. Digital Identity & KYC Passive Income | الهوية الرقمية والدخل المباشر
*   **National Digital Wallet:** Every citizen holds a sovereign digital identity linked to the **Digital Rial (YRD)**.
*   **Automated Dividends:** Periodic energy revenues (every 3, 6, or 12 months) are deposited directly into citizen wallets, bypassing administrative middlemen.
*   **Citizen as a Guardian:** By giving citizens a direct stake in production, the system transforms them into "Guardians of the Wealth," protecting infrastructure from sabotage.

## ⚡ 3. Energy for Development | الطاقة مقابل التنمية
*   **Domestic Power Priority:** Global contracts (with Aramco, ExxonMobil, etc.) mandate a percentage of production be diverted to power local grids at subsidized "Social Pricing."
*   **Border Development:** Transforming border regions (e.g., Saada, Hajjah, Al-Jawf) into "Sovereign Economic Zones" through strategic grid connectivity with Saudi Arabia.

## 🎓 4. Human Capital & Governance | حوكمة الكوادر البشرية
*   **AI-Driven Employment:** Job placements in the energy sector are managed by **AWSAN AI** based on merit and competency (KPIs), eliminating nepotism.
*   **Knowledge Transfer:** Global partners are required to implement a "3-for-1" training ratio (3 locals trained for every 1 foreign expert).
