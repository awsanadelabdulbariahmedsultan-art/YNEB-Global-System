## 🚢 1. Al-Dhabbah Terminal: The Crude Oil Command Center
*   **Strategic Importance:** Historically the main gateway for Yemen's crude oil exports. Under YNEB, it is upgraded with massive storage tanks and automated loading berths.
*   **Partnership & Tech:** 
    *   **Aramco (KSA) & Reliance (India):** Leading the engineering upgrades and logistical integration with regional/global markets.
    *   **Halliburton (USA):** Digitizing the terminal operations for real-time monitoring via **AWSAN AI**.
*   **Social Link:** Fuel produced here is supplied to **Socotra** at "Sovereign Social Prices" to ensure island energy security.

## ❄️ 2. Balhaf LNG Plant: The Hard Currency Engine
*   **Role:** Yemen’s primary facility for Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) exports, critical for national liquidity and World Bank-linked funds.
*   **Operational Conditions:** Balhaf operates under the "Big 5 Alliance" umbrella to ensure geopolitical neutrality and security.
*   **International Partners:**
    *   **ExxonMobil (USA) & ADNOC (UAE):** Leading the investment and resumption of global exports.
    *   **TotalEnergies & GE Energy:** Managing technical maintenance and power generation efficiency.

## 💰 3. Sovereign Financial Integration
*   **Sovereign Digital Settlements:** All export transactions at these terminals are executed via the **Digital Rial (YRD)** and the **AST (Sultan Token)** system.
*   **Asset Tokenization:** Investors can acquire **AST tokens** directly linked to the production capacity of these hubs, providing immediate liquidity for further infrastructure expansion.

## 🧠 4. AWSAN AI Governance
*   **Automated Auditing:** Every barrel and cubic meter exported is tracked on the blockchain to prevent revenue leakage.
*   **Regional Impact:** Revenues are algorithmically distributed to fund development in **Sanaa, Taiz, and Amran**, as well as the coastal regions.
